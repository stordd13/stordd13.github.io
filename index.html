About

Turning data into decisions

I'm a Data Scientist & ML Engineer based in Paris, with a strong background in applied mathematics and computer science. Over the past few years I've delivered data and ML projects across banking, healthcare and payments — from fraud scoring and operational-risk analysis to synthetic time-series generation and clinical event prediction.

I like owning the full lifecycle: exploring and cleaning data, engineering features, building machine learning and deep learning models, and shipping them to production with solid MLOps practices — testing, CI/CD, containers and monitoring.

Outside of work, I build ambitious side projects — an automated crypto trading system and a large-scale ML research platform — to keep sharpening my data engineering and applied-ML skills.
